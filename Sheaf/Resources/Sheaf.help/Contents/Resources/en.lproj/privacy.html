Sheaf and your privacy

What stays on your Mac, and the few things that leave it.

Sheaf has no account, no server, no ads and no analytics, and sends no crash reports. It is not allowed to connect to the internet at all: macOS keeps it in a sandbox without network access. Your documents are opened, changed and saved on your Mac, in the places you choose.

What Sheaf keeps on your Mac

  • Signatures you save, encrypted, with the key in your Keychain.
  • Flows you make, and your settings.
  • macOS keeps the list of recent documents and the versions of each document, as for any app.

Your name in the PDFs you mark up

Notes, highlights, drawings and signatures you add carry your name as their author, as in other PDF apps, and anyone you send the PDF to can see it. Change the name, or leave it empty to add none, in Sheaf ▸ Settings… ▸ General ▸ Name on notes and markup. Document Info shows the document's own author, which is separate.

Apple Intelligence

  • Summaries, answers, picture descriptions, Auto Crop, Remove Background and Super Resolution run on your Mac. The model for Super Resolution is downloaded from Apple once, after you agree.
  • Writing Tools and Image Playground are Apple's, and follow what you chose for Apple Intelligence in System Settings. Depending on those choices, Writing Tools can use Apple's Private Cloud Compute, or ChatGPT if you turned that on.
  • What Apple Intelligence writes is marked as such: a note made from a summary or a description says so and is signed "Apple Intelligence", and a picture from Image Playground carries the standard metadata for pictures made by a generative model.

Mail

When you reply to a PDF that came from Mail, Sheaf asks Mail, with the permission macOS asked you for, for the message the PDF came with, so that the reply quotes it. The draft and the copy of the PDF made for Mail are removed a few minutes later. Nothing is sent until you send it in Mail.
