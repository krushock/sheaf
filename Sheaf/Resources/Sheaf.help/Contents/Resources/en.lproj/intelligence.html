Sheaf Help

Apple Intelligence

Summaries, answers, Writing Tools and smarter pictures, on this Mac.

On a Mac with Apple Intelligence turned on, Sheaf uses the models that are part of macOS. They run on your Mac: Sheaf sends your documents nowhere. Writing Tools and Image Playground are Apple's own and follow the choices you made for them in System Settings.

Summary and key points

  • Click Summary in the toolbar, or choose Tools ▸ Summarize, to open the Summary panel on the right. ⌃⌘U shows and hides it.
  • Summary says in a few sentences what the document is about. Key Points lists what matters most.
  • The menu at the bottom chooses what is summed up: the whole document, the page you are reading, or the text you selected.
  • A long document is read a part at a time, which takes a while: a few minutes for a hundred pages of dense text. The panel shows how far it has come, and Stop ends it.
  • Copy the result, or put it on the page as a note with Add as Note.

Ask about the document

  • In the Summary panel, click Ask and type a question, such as "When does the lease end?".
  • The answer comes from the document's text. The pages it is from are named under it: click one to go there.
  • When the document does not seem to say, the answer says so rather than guessing.
The model on your Mac is small. It can misread a figure, miss a passage or leave something out, more so in long documents and in languages other than the main ones it knows. Check what matters against the pages. A scanned document has to have its text recognized before it can be read.

Writing Tools

  • While you edit text on a page, or type in a note or text box, Edit ▸ Writing Tools and the right-click menu proofread and rewrite it, as in Pages.
  • With text selected on a page that you are not editing, Writing Tools can summarize it or list its key points in a panel of its own.

Pictures

  • Image Playground: Edit ▸ Insert ▸ Image Playground… makes a picture and puts it on the page. Selected text is offered as what the picture is about, and a selected picture as what to make it from.
  • Super Resolution, in the options panel for a selected picture, gives it four times the pixels and sharpens it. It is for pictures up to 1920 pixels on a side without see-through parts. The first time, macOS may ask to download what it needs from Apple.
  • Auto Crop finds the part of a picture the eye goes to and masks the rest.
  • Remove Background keeps the subject of a photo and makes the rest see-through.
  • Describe says in a sentence or two what a picture shows. Copy the description or add it to the page as a note.

Title and keywords

In File ▸ Document Info…, Suggest fills in the title, subject and keywords that are empty, from how the document starts. Nothing is saved until you click Save.
