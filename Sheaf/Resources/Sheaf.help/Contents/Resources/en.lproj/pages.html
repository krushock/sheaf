Arrange pages

Reorder, rotate, add, remove and split pages.

Click Organize Pages in the toolbar, or press ⌥⌘O, to see every page in a grid.

  • Drag pages to reorder them. Select several with ⇧-click, ⌘-click or by dragging a box around them.
  • Drag PDFs and pictures in from the Finder to add them where you drop them. Drag pages out to the Finder to make a new PDF from them.
  • ⌘L and ⌘R rotate. Delete removes. Cut, Copy and Paste work between Sheaf windows.
  • Edit ▸ Insert adds a blank page, pages from a file, or pages from a scanner. A blank page can be the size of any page the document has, a common paper such as A4, A3 or US Letter, or, with Other Size…, any size either way up.
  • Tools ▸ Pages ▸ Split Down the Middle cuts each selected page in two, for scans of open books.
Removing a page also removes the bookmarks and links that pointed to it.
