Fill in and build forms

Fill fields, or add them to a form that has none.

Fill in

  • Click a field and type. Tab and ⇧Tab move between fields. Click checkboxes and choices to switch them.
  • Date fields show a calendar. Signature fields show your saved signatures.
  • Tools ▸ Form ▸ Highlight Fields tints the fields so they are easy to find.

Build

  • Take the Form fields tool in the strip at the side of the pages, or press ⇧⌘M. Choose a kind of field in the panel beside it and drag it out on the page, or click once for the usual size.
  • Detect Fields (the wand) makes fields from the boxes and lines printed on the page.
  • Select a field to rename it, set its look, or list a dropdown's options in the options panel.

Finish

  • Tools ▸ Form ▸ Flatten Form… makes what is filled in part of the pages, so it can no longer be changed.
  • Tools ▸ Form ▸ Reset Form… empties every field.
