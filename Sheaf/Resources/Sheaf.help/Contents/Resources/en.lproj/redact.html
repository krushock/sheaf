Remove information for good

Redaction takes text and pictures out of the file, not just out of sight.

Covering something with a black rectangle leaves it in the file, where anyone can copy it out. Redaction removes it.

  1. Click Edit in the toolbar and choose the Redact tool (the solid rectangle).
  2. Drag over what should go. Each mark is a red frame you can still see through, move, resize and delete.
  3. To mark every email address, phone number, card number or search result at once, use the wand menu.
  4. Click the eye to see the page as it will look.
  5. Click Apply…. Text, pictures and drawings under the marks are removed and black boxes are drawn in their place.
  6. Save.
Undo still brings the content back until you close the document. Copies of the file made before, and versions macOS keeps under File ▸ Revert To, still hold the original. Send the saved file, or a duplicate of it, not an older copy.

On a scanned page, a mark removes the part of the picture underneath it.
