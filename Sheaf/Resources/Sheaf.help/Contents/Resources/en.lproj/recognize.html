Make scans searchable

Recognize the text in scanned pages and photos.

A scan is a picture of a page. Tools ▸ Recognize Text… reads the writing in it and lays the words over the picture invisibly, so you can search, select and copy.

  • Recognition happens on your Mac. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • The page looks exactly as before, on screen and in print.
  • Pages that already have text are skipped.
