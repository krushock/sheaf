Sheaf Help

Mark up and sign

Highlight, comment, draw, stamp and sign.

Click Markup in the toolbar, or press ⇧⌘A, to show the markup tools.

Mark text

  • Choose Highlight, Underline or Strikethrough, then drag across text. With text already selected, clicking the tool marks it at once.

Add notes, text and shapes

  • Note: click where it should go and type in the Format panel.
  • Text: drag out a box and type. Double-click it later to change the words.
  • Shapes and drawing: drag to draw a rectangle, ellipse, line or arrow, or draw freehand.
  • Link: drag a box, then give it a web address or a page in the Format panel.

Change what you added

  • Click to select, drag to move, drag a handle to resize. Arrow keys nudge; hold ⇧ for bigger steps. Delete removes.
  • The Format panel (⌥⌘I) sets color, line width, opacity, font and size.

Sign

  • Click Sign. Draw a signature with the trackpad, type one, or use a photo of one on white paper. Saved signatures stay in the list.
  • This places a picture of your signature. It is not a certificate-based digital signature.

Make marks permanent

  • Tools ▸ Flatten Annotations… makes highlights, notes, drawings and stamps part of the pages.
