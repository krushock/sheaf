Sheaf Help

Read a document

Open, move around, search and zoom.

Open

  • Double-click a PDF or picture in the Welcome window, drag one onto Sheaf's icon, or choose File ▸ Open… ⌘O.
  • Pictures open as one-page documents. A protected PDF asks for its password.
  • A PDF opened from a message in Mail can be sent back: File ▸ Reply to Sender… opens a reply in Mail with the PDF attached and the earlier message quoted. Sheaf also offers this when you save or close such a PDF after changing it. It asks Mail which message the PDF came with, so macOS asks once whether Sheaf may control Mail, and the message has to be still selected there.
  • To have PDFs or pictures open in Sheaf when you double-click them, choose it under Default Apps in Sheaf ▸ Settings… ▸ General: for PDF documents, for all pictures at once, or for each kind of picture. Sheaf asks about PDFs once, the first time it opens.

Move around

  • The sidebar shows thumbnails, the table of contents, or highlights and notes. Switch with the button at the left of the toolbar, or ⌥⌘2, ⌥⌘3, ⌥⌘4. ⌥⌘1 hides it.
  • Go ▸ Go to Page… ⌥⌘G jumps to a page by number. ⌘[ and ⌘] go back and forward through where you have been.
  • View switches between continuous scrolling ⌘1, one page ⌘2 and two pages ⌘3.

Search

  • ⌘F puts the cursor in the search field. Results are listed in the sidebar; ⌘G and ⇧⌘G step through them.
  • A scanned document has no text to search until you recognize its text.

Zoom

  • The zoom floats at the foot of the tools: + and −, and the percentage between them is a menu with zoom levels, Fit Width and Fit Page. The View menu switches between continuous scrolling, one page and two pages.
  • Pinch, or use ⌘+ and ⌘−. ⌘0 is actual size, ⌘9 fits the width, ⌘8 fits the whole page.
