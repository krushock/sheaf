Compare two versions

See what changed between two documents.

File ▸ Compare Documents…, or Tools ▸ Compare with… ⇧⌘K from an open document.

  • The original is on the left and the revised version on the right. Removed words are tinted red, added words green. An orange frame marks a place that looks different though its words are the same, such as a changed picture.
  • The sidebar lists every difference. Click one, or press ⌘↓ and ⌘↑, to go to it on both sides.
  • The two sides scroll and zoom together. A page added or removed in the middle does not throw off the pages after it.
  • Export Report… saves the list of differences as a PDF.
